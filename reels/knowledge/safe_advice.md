# Safe free-advice facts (the ONLY numbers/claims allowed in the "free advice" rows)

Everything here is general public-health advice (WHO / CDC / NHS / ADA level). Use it, do not go beyond it.
If you want a fact that is not here, use plain qualitative advice without a number, or skip it. Never present advice as treatment.
Spoken/on-screen text never contains digits: write numbers in words (сад ва панҷоҳ, ду дақиқа, ҳафт соат ...).

## Numbers you may use
| Topic | Statement | Source level |
|---|---|---|
| Physical activity | adults: at least 150 minutes of moderate activity per week («сад ва панҷоҳ дақиқа ҳар ҳафта») | WHO |
| Daily walking | «ҳадди аққал сӣ дақиқа сайру гашт» is fine as a habit suggestion | general |
| Sleep | adults: seven hours or more at night («ҳафт соат ё бештар») | AASM / NSF |
| Fruit & vegetables | at least four hundred grams a day, about five portions («панҷ қисм») | WHO |
| Free sugars | keep sugary drinks/sweets low; under ten percent of daily energy | WHO |
| Salt | under five grams a day (about one teaspoon) | WHO |
| Hand washing | with soap and water, at least twenty seconds (WHO full technique is longer; "at least" is safe) | CDC/WHO |
| Tooth brushing | twice a day, two minutes each time; replace the brush about every three months | ADA / NHS |
| Dental check-up | see a dentist regularly, about once or twice a year | ADA |
| Healthy weight loss pace | about half a kilogram to one kilogram per week at most; slow is better | NHS |
| Caffeine | avoid strong tea/coffee in the afternoon and evening if sleep is poor | general |
| Screens | put the phone away about one hour before bed | sleep-hygiene guidance |

| Perfume longevity | only as the manufacturer states: up to two-three days depending on skin and clothes | channel text |

## Qualitative advice that is always safe
- Eat slowly, chew well, regular meals, smaller portions, less fried/very fatty/very salty food, more vegetables, fruit, legumes, whole grains, dairy such as qatiq.
- Drink water through the day; limit sweet drinks.
- Fixed bed/wake times, dark, cool, quiet room, light dinner, daylight and a short walk in the morning, slow deep breathing, writing worries on paper.
- Wash hands before eating, after toilet, after being outside or touching animals; wash vegetables/fruit; cook meat and fish thoroughly; drink clean (boiled/treated) water; keep nails short; wash bedding regularly.
- Rinse mouth with water after meals, clean between the teeth, clean the tongue, limit sugary snacks, do not smoke.
- Perfume: apply to clean, slightly moist skin on pulse points (wrists, neck), do not rub wrists together, keep away from heat and sunlight, a little is enough, test on skin before buying.
- See a doctor: symptoms that are severe, last long, keep coming back, come with weight loss, blood, fever or pain; children; pregnancy; chronic illness; taking medicines.

## Must-say lines (use the Tajik wording that fits the topic)
- Symptoms never prove an infection or a disease; only an examination/test decides («Нишонаҳо танҳо ташхис нестанд; сабабро духтур ва озмоиш муайян мекунад»).
- For long-lasting or worrying symptoms: «ба духтур муроҷиат кунед».
- Product line is always introduced with «Мувофиқи маълумоти истеҳсолкунанда, ...» when it describes an effect, and never contradicts `products.json`.

## Myths you may correct (short, factual)
- Eating sweets does not cause intestinal worms; infection comes from worm eggs/larvae in dirty hands, unwashed food, soil, dirty water, undercooked meat/fish.
- You do not need a strict diet to manage weight; slow steady change works better.
- Brushing harder does not clean better; two minutes with a soft brush is enough.

## Never
- Dosages, "take N times a day", children's dosing, comparisons with medicines, prices, discounts.
- Disease names as indications for a product (gastritis, prostatitis, depression, parasites...).
- "Guaranteed", "100%", "quickly", "cures", "removes", "kills", "detox", before/after promises.
- Sexual or intimate wording of any kind (men's-wellness videos stay about sleep, stress, movement, food, check-ups).
